Rental FAQs

Common questions from our tenants.

What is the standard lease term?

Most of our long-term rentals require a 12-month lease, but we also have 3-month and 6-month options available for specific properties.

Are utilities included in the rent?

It depends on the property. Serviced apartments usually include water, electricity, and internet, while standard homes typically require tenants to set up their own utilities.

Do I need to pay a security deposit?

Yes, we generally require a security deposit equivalent to one month's rent, which is refundable at the end of your tenancy, subject to a property inspection.

Are pets allowed?

Many of our standalone houses are pet-friendly. However, apartments have strict body corporate rules regarding pets. Please filter for "Pet-Friendly" when searching.
