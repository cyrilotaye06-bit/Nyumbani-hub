Buying FAQs

Everything you need to know about purchasing property through us.

Do you help with property valuation?

Yes, we have certified in-house valuers who can give you an accurate market price for any property you wish to buy or sell.

What legal documents do I need to buy property?

You will need your national ID or passport, KRA PIN, passport-size photographs, and funds for the deposit. We provide a legal team to handle the title deed transfer.

Can non-citizens buy property in Kenya?

Yes, non-citizens can buy commercial and residential property on leasehold (usually 99 years). Agricultural land is restricted, but our legal team can guide you through exemptions.

Are the property prices negotiable?

Most of our listed prices are negotiable to some extent. Our team can help negotiate on your behalf to secure the best deal possible with the seller.
